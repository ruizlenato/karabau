Karabau is an open-source Android client for Karakeep, built entirely with Jetpack Compose.

Use it to keep your bookmarks in one place, with a native mobile experience focused on speed and simplicity.

Features:
- Browse your complete bookmark feed
- Search bookmarks quickly
- Manage tags and lists
- Open bookmark details with quick actions (share, favorite, archive, delete)
- Save links directly from Android share/process text flows
- Secure credential storage on device

Karabau connects to your own Karakeep server and helps you organize links from anywhere.

Minimum Android version: 12 (API 31)
